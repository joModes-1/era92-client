## Full description
92 CarWash makes getting your car washed simple. Scan the QR code at a branch or pick one near you, choose your vehicle and wash, and you're in the queue. Watch your car's progress in the app and get a notification the moment it's ready.

FOR DRIVERS
• Book in seconds: scan the branch QR code or find a branch near you.
• Pay your way: MTN Mobile Money, Airtel Money or card, securely through PesaPal. Or pay cash at the bay.
• Earn points on every wash and use them to pay for your next one.
• Two cars, one price: share the offer with a friend and wash two cars for the price of one.
• Follow every car you have on the go, and keep your receipts in one place.

FOR CAR-WASH TEAMS
• Workers see their queue, take the next car, and finish a wash with one slide.
• Fair job sharing, so cars are spread evenly between workers.
• A wallet that shows commission earned and cash to hand in, plus a weekly points leaderboard.
• Managers confirm cash handovers and see the day's takings, who is working, and what needs attention.
• Owners see every branch in one place: daily reports, cash variance, staff, prices and customers.

Sign in with just your phone number. No passwords to remember.
