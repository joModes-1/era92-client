Privacy Policy

92 CarWash, a product of Era92 · Last updated: 26 October 2026

This policy explains what the 92 CarWash app ("the app") collects, why, and what control you have. By using the app you agree to it.

What we collect

  • Phone number. You sign in with your mobile number and a one-time code we send by SMS. This is your account.
  • Name. Where available, the name registered to your mobile money number; otherwise a name we generate. You are not asked to type one.
  • Your car-wash activity. The branch, service, vehicle type, number plate and colour you enter when you book; the status and price of each wash; and the points you earn and spend.
  • Payments. When you pay with mobile money or card, you pay on PesaPal's page. We receive the payment status, amount and a reference from PesaPal. We never see or store your card number, PIN or mobile money PIN.
  • Location (optional). Only while you use the app, to show branches near you. You can refuse and still pick a branch by scanning its QR code.
  • Camera (optional). Only to scan a branch's QR code. Images are not stored or uploaded.
  • Notifications (optional). A device token, so we can tell you when your car is ready or a payment arrives.
  • Staff accounts. For employees using the app at work we also hold their role, branch, shift times, cash handovers and commission figures.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account secure.
  • To book, track and settle your wash, and to give you points and receipts.
  • To collect payment and prevent mistakes or fraud.
  • To show the washer and manager the information they need to do the job.
  • To send the notifications and sign-in codes described above. We do not send marketing messages without your consent.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your data. We share only what is needed with services that help us run the app:

  • Africa's Talking – delivers sign-in codes by SMS (receives your phone number).
  • PesaPal – processes mobile money and card payments.
  • MTN and Airtel – may be asked for the name registered to a mobile money number.
  • Expo / Google (Firebase) – deliver push notifications.
  • The car-wash business you book with – sees your bookings, name and phone number so it can serve you.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your account and wash history while your account is active, and for as long afterwards as needed for accounting, dispute and legal purposes. Sign-in codes expire within minutes and are deleted.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and held on access-controlled servers. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information.

Your choices and rights

  • Turn off location, camera or notifications at any time in your phone's settings.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data, as the Data Protection and Privacy Act, 2019 of Uganda allows. Email us from the address below with the phone number on your account.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.
